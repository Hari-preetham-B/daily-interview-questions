# 2026-11-07 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a situation where you had to negotiate with a stakeholder to reduce the scope of a feature due to budget constraints, while still ensuring the core user needs were met.

## Hint
Consider how you identified priorities, communicated constraints, and worked with the stakeholder to find a compromise.

## Answer
In a recent project, the product manager requested a real‑time analytics dashboard, but our sprint budget could only support a simplified version. I first mapped the user journey and identified the three most critical metrics that drove business decisions. I scheduled a short meeting with the stakeholder, presented the cost estimates, and explained the trade‑off between a full dashboard and a lightweight report. Together we agreed to postpone advanced visualizations to the next release and focus on delivering the core metrics with a clean interface. The stakeholder approved the reduced scope, and we delivered the MVP on time, keeping the budget intact while still meeting the primary user needs.
