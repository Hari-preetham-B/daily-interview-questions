# 2026-11-15 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a scenario where you had to manage a technical debt issue that was impacting the team's velocity, and explain how you decided whether to address it immediately or postpone it.

## Hint
Consider the trade‑offs between short‑term delivery and long‑term maintainability, and how you might involve stakeholders in that decision.

## Answer
In a recent sprint, I noticed a legacy authentication module was causing frequent bugs and slowing down feature integration. I quantified the impact by measuring defects linked to that module and time spent debugging, then weighed it against the sprint goal of delivering a new user‑profile feature. After discussing with product and engineering leads, we allocated a small portion of the next sprint to refactor the module while deferring non‑critical enhancements. This reduced the defect rate by 30% and kept the main delivery on schedule. I documented the technical debt in the backlog to keep it visible for future planning.
