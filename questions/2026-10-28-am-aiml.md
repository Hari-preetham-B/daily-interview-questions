## Question
Compare L1 (Lasso) and L2 (Ridge) regularization in linear regression. How does each affect the model coefficients, and in what situations would you prefer one over the other?

## Hint
Think about how the penalty term shapes the solution space and what happens to small coefficients.

## Answer
L1 regularization adds the absolute value of the coefficients to the loss function, which can drive some coefficients exactly to zero, producing a sparse model. L2 regularization adds the squared values of the coefficients, which shrinks all coefficients towards zero but rarely makes them exactly zero, leading to a dense solution. You would prefer L1 when feature selection is important or when you expect many irrelevant features; you would choose L2 when you want a stable solution with many small non‑zero coefficients, especially when features are correlated.
