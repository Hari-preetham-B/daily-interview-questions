## Question
Explain the purpose of batch normalization in deep neural networks. Include its mathematical formulation, how it normalizes activations during training, the role of running estimates during inference, and how it impacts gradient flow and training speed.

## Hint
Think about how normalizing the distribution of activations can make the loss landscape smoother and what parameters the layer learns to preserve the representational power.

## Answer
Batch Normalization (BN) rescales and re-centers activations to have zero mean and unit variance per mini‑batch:

â_i = (a_i - μ_B)/√(σ_B²+ε),

y_i = γ·â_i + β,
where μ_B and σ_B² are batch mean and variance, γ and β are learnable scale and shift. During training, BN uses the current batch statistics; during inference it uses running estimates of μ and σ accumulated over training. By keeping activations well‑scaled, BN reduces internal covariate shift, allowing larger learning rates, faster convergence, and more stable gradients. The γ, β parameters let the network recover any necessary scaling or bias that would otherwise be lost due to normalization.
