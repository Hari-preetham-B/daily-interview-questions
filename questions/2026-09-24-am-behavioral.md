# 2026-09-24 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a time when you had to adapt your communication style to effectively collaborate with a colleague or stakeholder who had a different technical background than yours. How did you adjust your approach, and what was the result?

## Hint
Consider the audience’s level of technical knowledge and how you might use analogies or visual aids.

## Answer
I once worked on a project with a product manager who had little coding experience. I began by explaining our technical constraints using simple analogies, such as comparing our database latency to a traffic jam, and used diagrams instead of code snippets. I also asked clarifying questions to gauge her understanding and encouraged her to voice concerns. As a result, she was able to provide clearer requirements, which reduced rework and kept the project on schedule.
