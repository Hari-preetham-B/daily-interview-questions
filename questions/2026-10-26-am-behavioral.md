# 2026-10-26 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Tell me about a time when you had to make a decision to abandon or pivot a feature that was no longer aligned with the company’s product strategy. How did you handle stakeholder communication and what was the outcome?

## Hint
Think about how you assessed the feature’s alignment, gathered data, and communicated the change to stakeholders while preserving trust.

## Answer
I first reviewed usage metrics and feedback to confirm the feature’s low impact, then presented a concise data‑driven slide deck to the product and engineering leads showing the opportunity cost. I proposed a pivot that focused on high‑value user pain points and secured buy‑in by highlighting the potential revenue uplift. After aligning the roadmap, we reallocated resources, delivered the new feature on schedule, and increased overall user satisfaction by 15%.
