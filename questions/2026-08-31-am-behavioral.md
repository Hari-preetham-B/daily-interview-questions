# 2026-08-31 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Tell me about a time when you received difficult or constructive feedback on a project or assignment. How did you handle the situation, and what changes did you make based on that feedback?

## Hint
Use the STAR framework. Emphasize emotional maturity, active listening, and the concrete actions you took to act on the feedback rather than becoming defensive.

## Answer
A strong answer follows the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) method. Begin by describing a realistic scenario, such as receiving critical feedback during a code review or project presentation. Highlight that you listened actively without getting defensive, asked clarifying questions to fully understand the critique, and thanked the reviewer. Then, detail the specific steps you took to correct the issue—such as refactoring code, improving documentation, or adopting a new workflow. Conclude with the positive outcome, explaining how the feedback improved the final deliverable and helped you grow technically or professionally.
