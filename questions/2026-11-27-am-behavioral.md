# 2026-11-27 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a situation where you introduced a new process or tool to your team that required everyone to change their workflow, but initially met resistance. How did you handle the pushback, ensure adoption, and measure success?

## Hint
Consider the steps you took to communicate benefits, address concerns, and demonstrate value through metrics or small wins.

## Answer
I identified a bottleneck in our deployment pipeline and proposed adopting a continuous integration tool. Initially, teammates were hesitant because it meant extra steps in their daily routine. I organized a short demo showing how the tool could catch bugs early and save time in the long run, and then ran a pilot with a single feature branch. I gathered feedback, tweaked the configuration, and provided a cheat sheet for the most common commands. After a week, our build failure rate dropped by 30% and the average release cycle shortened by 20%, which convinced the team to fully adopt the new process.
