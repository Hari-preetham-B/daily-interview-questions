# 2026-10-22 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a time when you joined a team mid-project and had to quickly earn the trust of your teammates to contribute effectively.

## Hint
Consider specific actions you took to understand the team's dynamics, show reliability, and align with their goals.

## Answer
When I joined the mobile app team two weeks into the development cycle, I first spent a day shadowing teammates to understand their workflow and the codebase. I scheduled brief one‑on‑one meetings to ask clarifying questions and listen to their pain points. I volunteered to tackle a backlog item that was blocking multiple features, delivered it ahead of schedule, and documented the process clearly. By sharing my progress in daily stand‑ups and offering help on their tasks, I demonstrated reliability and a collaborative mindset. Over the next sprint, the team trusted me with more responsibility, and we delivered the release on time.
