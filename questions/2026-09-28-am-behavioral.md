# 2026-09-28 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Tell me about a time when you had to manage a stakeholder with unrealistic expectations about project delivery. How did you handle the situation and what was the outcome?

## Hint
Consider how you assessed the situation, communicated the constraints, and negotiated a realistic plan.

## Answer
During my internship at a fintech startup, a product manager expected a fully functional payment gateway within one week, which conflicted with the technical complexity and our current sprint commitments. I scheduled a brief meeting to present a realistic timeline, outlining the required steps, potential risks, and impact on quality. By providing concrete data on development effort and demonstrating a phased rollout plan, I helped align expectations with what the team could deliver. The stakeholder agreed to a two‑week timeline for a minimum viable version, which allowed us to meet quality standards and avoid a rushed release.
