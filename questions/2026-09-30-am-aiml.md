## Question
Explain how dropout acts as a regularization technique in neural networks. Include the mathematical expectation of the output during training and inference, and describe why dropout can be interpreted as model averaging over many subnetworks.

## Hint
Think about the scaling factor applied to the activations during training and what the network sees during inference.

## Answer
Dropout randomly zeroes a fraction p of activations in a layer during training. The remaining activations are scaled by 1/(1‑p) so that the expected activation value stays the same: E[dropout(a)]=a. During inference the full network is used without dropping units; the weights are effectively scaled by (1‑p). This scaling ensures consistent output distribution between training and test. Because each training step samples a different sub‑network, the final model can be viewed as an ensemble of 2^n subnetworks averaged together, reducing variance and helping to prevent overfitting.
