# 2026-10-10 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a situation where you had to change your working style to accommodate a teammate who had a different approach to problem-solving. How did you adapt, and what was the outcome?

## Hint
Consider how you used communication and flexibility to blend different working habits.

## Answer
In a recent project, my teammate preferred a very analytical, step‑by‑step debugging process, while I tend to jump straight into writing code and testing quickly. To accommodate this, I scheduled short daily syncs where we mapped out a shared plan before coding, and I made sure to document each step so she could review and suggest improvements. I also started using pair‑programming sessions to blend our styles, which reduced misunderstandings and improved the code quality. As a result, we completed the feature two days ahead of schedule and received positive feedback from the product owner.
