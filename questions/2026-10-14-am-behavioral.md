# 2026-10-14 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Tell me about a time when you noticed a teammate consistently arriving late to team meetings, and you had to address the issue without causing friction. How did you approach the conversation, what was the outcome, and what did you learn?

## Hint
Think about setting clear expectations and using a collaborative tone.

## Answer
I scheduled a short one‑to‑one, started by acknowledging their recent contributions, then shared my observations about the late arrivals and how it impacted the team’s flow. I asked if there were obstacles and offered to help find a solution. We agreed on a new meeting schedule that worked for both of us. The next week, the teammate started arriving on time, and the team’s meeting efficiency improved. I learned that addressing issues privately and collaboratively often leads to better outcomes than public criticism.
