# 2026-10-06 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a situation where you had to mediate a disagreement between two teammates about the prioritization of features for a release. How did you approach the discussion, what steps did you take to reach a consensus, and what was the outcome?

## Hint
Consider how you gathered input from both parties, identified shared goals, and used data or business impact to guide the decision.

## Answer
During a sprint planning meeting, two senior developers argued over whether to implement a new search feature or fix a critical bug. I organized a brief one-on-one with each to understand their concerns and then a joint session where we listed the business impact, customer feedback, and technical effort for each option. We used a simple scoring matrix to quantify risk vs. value, and agreed to address the bug first because it affected a larger user base. The release went on schedule, the bug was fixed, and the search feature was planned for the next sprint, improving team morale and product quality.
