# 2026-11-19 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Tell me about a time when you had to onboard a new team member whose skill set was very different from the rest of the team, and how you used their unique strengths to benefit the project.

## Hint
Consider the initial assessment you did and how you matched tasks to their abilities.

## Answer
During my second semester project, a new member joined with a strong background in data visualization but little experience in backend development. I first organized a short skill‑mapping session to understand their strengths and areas of interest. I then delegated the UI dashboard creation to them while I handled the API and database logic, ensuring that both parts of the project progressed concurrently. This division of labor not only kept the timeline on track but also resulted in a more polished and intuitive user interface, which earned us a higher grade and positive feedback from our professor.
