# 2026-11-03 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a time when you had to adjust your personal work schedule to support a teammate facing a critical personal emergency, while still meeting your own project deadlines. How did you prioritize tasks and communicate with stakeholders?

## Hint
Consider how you balanced empathy with accountability and the steps you took to ensure both personal and project responsibilities were addressed.

## Answer
In a recent project, a teammate had to leave unexpectedly due to a family emergency. I first assessed my own critical tasks and re‑scheduled non‑urgent work for a later date. I then offered to take over the teammate’s most time‑sensitive assignments, coordinating with the product owner to confirm priority. I kept stakeholders informed through a brief status update, explaining the shift in responsibilities and the expected impact on delivery. The project stayed on track, and the teammate appreciated the support and timely communication.
