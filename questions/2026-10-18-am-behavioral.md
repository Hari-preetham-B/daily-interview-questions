# 2026-10-18 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Tell me about a time when you worked with a remote team from a different cultural background, and you had to adjust your communication style to ensure clarity and maintain team cohesion.

## Hint
Think about how you identified differences in communication norms and what concrete steps you took to adapt.

## Answer
I was part of a cross‑continental sprint with developers in India and Brazil. I noticed that our weekly stand‑ups were often misinterpreted because of differing cultural norms around directness and timekeeping. I suggested a shared agenda template and brief written summaries after each meeting. I also encouraged using asynchronous channels for clarifications. As a result, the team reported higher clarity and we delivered the sprint on time.
