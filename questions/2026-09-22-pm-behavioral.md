# 2026-09-22 PM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a situation where you worked with a team spread across multiple time zones. How did you coordinate tasks and maintain effective communication?

## Hint
Think about tools, scheduling, and documentation you used to keep everyone aligned.

## Answer
In my final year project I collaborated with teammates in India, Germany, and the US. We set up a shared Kanban board on Trello, assigning clear owners and due dates. Weekly sprint meetings were rotated so each region could attend at a reasonable hour. We used Slack for quick questions and a shared Google Docs notebook for meeting minutes, ensuring everyone could reference decisions asynchronously. By combining these practices, we reduced miscommunication and delivered the prototype on schedule.
