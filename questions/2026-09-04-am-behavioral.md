# 2026-09-04 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Describe a situation where you had to work with ambiguous requirements or faced a sudden, unexpected change in project scope. How did you navigate the uncertainty, and what was the final outcome?

## Hint
Focus on how you proactively sought clarity, communicated with stakeholders or teammates, and adapted your technical strategy without losing momentum.

## Answer
A strong response uses the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to demonstrate adaptability, proactive communication, and problem-solving skills. Start by explaining the context and the specific ambiguity or scope change encountered. Detail the concrete actions you took to resolve the uncertainty, such as interviewing stakeholders to clarify requirements, breaking down vague tasks into manageable milestones, or building a rapid prototype to validate assumptions. Conclude by sharing the successful outcome, quantifiable metrics if available, and key takeaways regarding how you handle shifting priorities in a software development lifecycle.
