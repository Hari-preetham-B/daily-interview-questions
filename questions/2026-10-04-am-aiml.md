## Question
Explain the concept of teacher forcing in training sequence‑to‑sequence models. Why is it useful, what are the potential pitfalls, and how can scheduled sampling mitigate these pitfalls?

## Hint
Think about what the decoder receives during training versus inference.

## Answer
Teacher forcing is a training technique where the decoder of a seq2seq model is fed the true previous token instead of its own prediction. It speeds up convergence and provides strong supervision, but it creates a discrepancy between training and inference because at test time the decoder must rely on its own outputs. This can lead to exposure bias, where errors accumulate and degrade performance. Scheduled sampling gradually replaces ground‑truth tokens with model predictions during training, thereby exposing the model to its own mistakes and reducing the mismatch between training and inference.
