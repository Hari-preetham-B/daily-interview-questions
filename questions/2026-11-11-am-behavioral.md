# 2026-11-11 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Tell me about a time you participated in a hackathon or coding sprint where the team had to deliver a working prototype within a 48‑hour deadline. How did you quickly assign roles, manage your own tasks, and coordinate with teammates to ensure the project was completed on time?

## Hint
Focus on how you broke down the project, communicated expectations, and tracked progress under pressure.

## Answer
During a university hackathon, I volunteered to lead the frontend team. I started by sketching a high‑level feature map and then held a quick stand‑up to let each member express their strengths and interests. I delegated the UI design to a teammate with strong CSS skills, the API integration to another who was comfortable with Flask, and I took on the overall architecture and deployment. I set up a shared Kanban board and checked in every hour to monitor progress and adjust tasks as blockers arose. By maintaining clear communication, we finished a functional prototype 30 minutes before the deadline, and the project earned the best demo award.
