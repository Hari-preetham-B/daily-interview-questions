# 2026-10-30 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Tell me about a time when you had to give a teammate difficult feedback about their work. How did you prepare for the conversation, what approach did you use, and what was the outcome for both the teammate and the project?

## Hint
Consider the balance between honesty and empathy, and think about concrete steps you took to frame the feedback constructively.

## Answer
I identified that a teammate’s code had several anti‑pattern issues that were impacting the build stability. Before the meeting I collected specific examples and potential solutions, and I chose a private setting to avoid public embarrassment. During the conversation I started by acknowledging their effort, then described the impact of the issues on the project, and suggested actionable steps they could take. I also offered to pair‑program to review changes. The teammate appreciated the respectful tone, adopted the suggested practices, and the overall build reliability improved within a week. The experience strengthened our collaboration and set clearer coding standards for the team.
