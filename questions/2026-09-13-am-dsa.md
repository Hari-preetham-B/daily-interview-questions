# 2026-09-13 AM — DSA (Hard)

## Question
Given `n` non-negative integers representing an elevation map where the width of each bar is `1`, compute how much water it can trap after raining.

## Hint
Think about how much water a single bar can hold. It depends on the maximum height of bars to its left and right. Can you optimize finding these max heights using a two-pointer technique?

## Answer
We can solve this using a two-pointer approach in O(n) time and O(1) space. Maintain two pointers, `left` at index 0 and `right` at index `n - 1`, along with `left_max` and `right_max` tracking the peak heights from both ends. At each step, compare `left_max` and `right_max`. If `left_max < right_max`, the water trapped at `left` is determined by `left_max - height[left]`; increment `left` and update `left_max`. Otherwise, the water trapped at `right` is determined by `right_max - height[right]`; decrement `right` and update `right_max`. Repeat until `left` meets `right` and sum the trapped water.
