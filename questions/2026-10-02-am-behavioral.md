# 2026-10-02 AM — Behavioral (Medium)

## Question
Can you describe a time when you successfully convinced your team to adopt a new technology or process that they were initially hesitant about?

## Hint
Focus on a situation where you identified a gap, gathered evidence, and communicated benefits to get buy‑in.

## Answer
In my sophomore year, our project suffered from slow build times due to an outdated scripting approach. I researched modern CI/CD tools, created a proof‑of‑concept pipeline that cut build time by 40%, and prepared a short presentation highlighting the productivity gains and cost savings. I addressed concerns about the learning curve by offering a short training session and documenting the setup. After a team meeting, we voted to adopt the new pipeline, which we fully integrated by the end of the semester, resulting in faster releases and higher morale.
