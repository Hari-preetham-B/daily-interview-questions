# 2026-08-23 AM — Behavioral (Easy)

## Question
Describe a situation where you had a strong technical disagreement with a teammate during a group project. How did you handle it, and what was the outcome?

## Hint
Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) and focus on data-driven decision-making, objective communication, and prioritizing project success over personal ego.

## Answer
To answer this question effectively, use the STAR framework. Start by setting the context of the project and clearly stating the conflict without speaking negatively about your teammate. Focus your 'Action' section on how you remained objective—for example, by benchmarking both technical approaches, listing pros and cons, or seeking input from a mentor or TA. Highlight your active listening skills and willingness to compromise. Conclude with the 'Result', demonstrating that the team reached a mutually agreed-upon solution, successfully delivered the project on time, and maintained a healthy professional relationship.
